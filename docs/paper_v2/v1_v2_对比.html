1. Introduction

删除Construction remains among the most hazardous industries worldwide. In China, hundreds of fatal construction accidents are officially reported each year, with collapse, fall-from-height, and hoisting incidents the most recurrent types. Each accident produces investigation reports and news narratives that record what happened, how it happened, and why. These texts constitute the largest and least exploited resource for construction safety research: they contain causal information accumulated at national scale over decades, yet they remain unstructured natural language that resists systematic analysis.

删除The bottleneck is conversion. Turning narratives into analyzable records has traditionally required manual reading and coding by domain experts, a process that does not scale to the volume of reports produced annually (Kong and Ahn, 2024). Rule-based and keyword-matching systems generalize poorly across the varied linguistic patterns of accident writing (Togan et al., 2025). Supervised classifiers such as BERT (Devlin et al., 2019) and its distilled variants improve coverage but demand substantial annotated corpora that are expensive to produce in this domain. Large language models have recently opened a different route: direct inference from unstructured text without task-specific training data (Brown et al., 2020; OpenAI, 2023). Prior studies have demonstrated LLM extraction of accident attributes from OSHA narratives (Ahmadi et al., 2024), HFACS classification of Korean fall accidents (Kim and Han, 2024), HFACS-guided causal inference on aviation reports (Liu et al., 2025a), and knowledge-graph construction from construction reports (Liu and Luo, 2025).

删除Three gaps persist across this literature. First, no existing system couples a causal taxonomy designed for the construction domain with an end-to-end pipeline that connects collection, extraction, causal coding, standardization, storage, and online access; prior work stops at extraction or at a static knowledge artifact. Second, the prompting strategies that drive LLM causal coding have been ranked on a single model (GPT-4o) in a single domain (aviation), leaving open whether the ranking generalizes across models — and the studies that employ domain-knowledge chain-of-thought prompts have not examined how the *phrasing* of embedded knowledge conditions the model's behavior. Third, the reliability of LLM annotations has not been formally validated against human experts in the construction domain with appropriate agreement statistics, so the substitution of human coders by LLMs rests on anecdote rather than measurement.

删除This paper addresses the three gaps with CAIEMS (Construction Accident Intelligent Extraction and Mining System) and the study surrounding it. CAIEMS is an LLM-driven pipeline that converts unstructured Chinese construction accident narratives into a structured, causally annotated, interactive online database of 2,623 records with 33 standardized fields and 17,364 evidence-bound causal codes. Its causal substrate is CAICS (Construction Accident Integrated Cause System), a five-layer, nineteen-category taxonomy purpose-built for construction accidents and grounded in our corpus. During the development of the causal encoder, we identified and remedied a prompt-design failure mode that we name the **Lexical Gatekeeping Effect**: when domain knowledge is embedded in a chain-of-thought prompt as conditional keyword checks, the model treats the listed keywords as mandatory gates rather than illustrative cues, and recall collapses on exactly the highest-frequency implicit causes. A two-step judgment remedy (explicit matching plus authorized inference) restores F1 from 0.503 to 0.656, a 30% recovery. In the same experiment, Few-Shot prompting achieved the highest F1 of 0.669, reversing the strategy ranking established by Liu et al. (2025a) on GPT-4o and demonstrating that prompt-strategy effectiveness is model-dependent. Finally, a Cohen's Kappa agreement study (n = 289) shows that the LLM's basic-field annotations fall within the human expert-to-expert agreement range on all six measured features (κ = 0.91–0.97).

3. System Design

删除### 3.1 Problem Formulation

删除We formalize the task of construction accident information extraction as follows. Let $T = \{t_1, t_2, \dots, t_N\}$ denote a corpus of unstructured accident report texts, where each $t_i$ is a free-form Chinese narrative of variable length (median 517 characters in our corpus). The goal is to transform each $t_i$ into a structured record $r_i = (b_i, c_i)$ consisting of two components: (1) a basic information vector $b_i \in \mathcal{B}$ covering $33$ pre-defined fields, including temporal attributes (date, time), spatial attributes (province, city, district, location detail), outcome attributes (accident type, severity, deaths, injuries, trapped count, direct economic loss), and contextual attributes (work task, equipment involved, material involved, worker type, building structure, construction phase, weather, and project participants); and (2) a causal annotation $c_i = \{(x_{ij}, e_{ij}, \iota_{ij})\}_{j=1}^{k_i}$, where each $x_{ij}$ is a causal code drawn from our CAICS taxonomy, $e_{ij}$ is the verbatim textual evidence supporting the code, and $\iota_{ij}$ is the inference chain connecting evidence to code. Each code is required to carry its evidence: a causal code without a quotable source sentence is treated as a hallucination by construction.

删除Three properties distinguish this formulation from prior work. First, extraction is evidence-bound: every causal code must be traceable to an explicit source sentence, which makes the resulting database auditable, a property absent from classification-only pipelines (Ahmadi et al., 2024; Kim and Han, 2024). Second, the output schema is field-standardized: five free-text fields (work task, equipment, material, worker type, building structure) are normalized into mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive (MECE) enumerations, so downstream statistical analysis operates on closed sets rather than open vocabularies. Third, the pipeline is end-to-end: it connects collection, filtering, extraction, coding, enrichment, and storage into a single operational system, in contrast to prior systems that terminate at extraction (Togan et al., 2025) or at a static knowledge artifact (Liu and Luo, 2025; Jiang et al., 2026).

删除Two design choices differentiate CAICS from adaptations of the aviation-derived HFACS taxonomy (Shappell and Wiegmann, 2000; Liu et al., 2025a). First, the F layer captures physical protection failures, which are endemic in construction (missing edge protection, unstable support systems) but have no natural home in HFACS, whose layers describe human and organizational performance in operational aviation. Second, the C layer records consequence amplification factors, a phenomenon frequently reported in Chinese construction accidents, where delayed reporting (C2) is itself a regulatory violation that compounds casualties by delaying external rescue. The taxonomy is deliberately small (nineteen categories) relative to HFACS-based extensions: category sparsity per code was a binding constraint observed in preliminary experiments, and the final size balances discriminative power against per-category statistical power.

删除**Taxonomy derivation.** CAICS was derived through corpus-driven induction under the causal leak model. We extracted causal statements from the accident corpus, clustered them into candidate categories by recurrent failure patterns, and consolidated the clusters into the five-layer structure, pruning categories whose corpus frequency fell below a statistical-power floor. Each retained category received an operational definition plus typical evidence keywords harvested from the corpus (Table 1 reports the definitions); the corpus frequency of each code is reported alongside its definition in the released operational definition table. Unlike these frameworks, CAICS was designed from the outset as a finite coding scheme with operational definitions, a format that LLM-based extraction requires.

新增Let $T = \{t_1, t_2, \dots, t_N\}$ denote a corpus of unstructured accident narratives (median 517 characters in our corpus). 新增The three tiers carry different epistemic commitments, and the database stores and reports them separately:

新增- **explicit**: the source text contains a passage that directly supports the code; $e_{ij}$ is a verbatim quotation. Only explicit codes are presented as evidence-backed in the Web interface. - **inferred**: the code is induced from the narrative by semantic paraphrase (e.g., a text stating "safety management inadequate" supports M1 without quoting any fixed keyword). The inference chain is recorded. - **prior**: the code follows from domain assumptions authorized in the prompt (e.g., a fatal accident is taken to imply deficient supervision even when the text is silent). 新增This scheme replaces the stronger claim, made in an earlier draft, that every code carries quotable evidence and that evidence-free codes are hallucinations by construction. Across the database, 14,784 annotations (90.6%) are explicit, 1,117 (6.8%) are inferred, and 422 (2.6%) are prior; the remainder of this paper reports totals and, where relevant, tier-level breakdowns.

新增#### 3.2.2 Collection schemes

新增**Web-collection track.** Twenty-five collectors acquire the web portion. Each implements a common interface and is registered in a central registry; the implementations fall into four families — one search-engine aggregator that issues predefined queries and admits results only from a domain whitelist; nineteen provincial emergency-management bureau collectors that parse HTML listing and detail pages and, in most cases, download and extract attached PDF investigation reports; two national-level collectors (Ministry of Emergency Management and MOHURD); and three auxiliary importers covering open-government datasets, a safety-news special-topic archive, and two published academic accident datasets.

新增HTTP retrieval uses a single client library across all collectors (httpx, timeouts of 15–60 s). HTML is parsed with BeautifulSoup, using lxml or the standard-library parser according to the source's markup. Main-text extraction prefers trafilatura where page structure permits, falls back to a BeautifulSoup pass that removes script, style, navigation, footer, and form elements, and falls back again to tag stripping with entity unescaping. Attached PDFs are extracted with PyMuPDF when a text layer is present and routed to a document-parsing service with OCR capability when it is not.

新增Query construction combines two mechanisms. The aggregator issues 17 predefined query templates covering collapse, fall from height, hoisting, excavation, scaffolding, electrocution, fire, poisoning, struck-by, and machinery, together with ministry-level report queries; per-record search enhancement (Section 3.4) later generates up to three additional queries from each accident's own place names and keywords. Admission of a search result is restricted to a whitelist of 15 news domains plus any `gov.cn` host, which keeps search-derived material inside the same source population as the targeted collectors.

新增Politeness and robustness measures are deliberately conventional: a fixed desktop browser user-agent, complete request headers including language preference and, for search-derived requests, a referer, a serial inter-request delay of 1–5 s per collector, and three retrieval attempts per URL with a two-second backoff. **We did not implement proxy rotation, user-agent rotation, JavaScript rendering, or CAPTCHA handling, and collection is entirely serial rather than distributed.** We state this explicitly because it bounds both the collection rate and the extendability of the current corpus: sources that require session handling or dynamic rendering fall outside the present scheme and are represented only insofar as their reports are mirrored on admitted sites.

新增**Document-digitization track.** Each volume is converted to text either by extracting its existing text layer or, for scanned volumes, through OCR document parsing, and is then segmented into individual cases. Two segmentation procedures are available. A rule-based splitter anchors on the structural heading convention these compilations use, and an LLM-based splitter returns a start marker and an end marker for each case, after which the cases are cut programmatically by string search rather than accepting model-generated character offsets — the resulting spans are therefore exactly recoverable from the source document and can be audited. Segments below a minimum length (50–100 characters, depending on the splitter) are discarded. Each retained case then enters the same cleaning, filtering, and coding pathway as a web record, so the two tracks converge before any information extraction takes place.

新增#### 3.2.3 Cleaning, normalization, and rule-based pre-extraction

新增Cleaning and the bulk of field extraction are performed by deterministic rules rather than by model calls. Three components operate in sequence on every candidate record.

新增The text cleaner removes boilerplate and rendering artifacts. Ten regular-expression patterns strip recurring site furniture — copyright notices, ICP filing numbers, technical-support lines, sponsorship lines, browser-compatibility notices, disclaimers, and privacy statements — and eight zero-width or invisible code points are deleted. HTML entities are unescaped and text is normalized to NFKC, which folds full-width forms onto their half-width equivalents; runs of whitespace collapse, and more than two consecutive newlines are reduced. Lines shorter than four characters that contain no digit are dropped, a filter that removes stray headings while preserving short lines carrying casualty figures. Documents longer than 50,000 characters are truncated, retaining the leading portion, since accident narratives state the salient facts early. The cleaner is applied identically to web text and to digitized case text.

新增A rule-based field extractor then applies ordered regular-expression patterns across eight field groups — date, location, casualties, accident type, severity, direct economic loss, contractor, and project name. Patterns are ordered by specificity and carry priority weights; for example, a fully specified date (`YYYY年M月D日`) outranks a year-month form, which outranks a month-day form recovered by context. Every extracted value passes plausibility checks (a year within 2000–2030, a death toll below 200, an injury count below 500). A weighted confidence score combines the presence of a date, a city, an accident type, and a death toll, with a small bonus for each additional field recovered.

新增A normalizer maps both extracted and free-text values onto controlled vocabularies: accident type onto one of twelve categories; severity onto the four statutory tiers defined by Chinese accident regulations — general, relatively major, major, and particularly major, at death-toll thresholds of 3, 10, and 30 — with an explicit severity phrase in the text taking precedence over the inferred tier; province and city onto canonical administrative names through a mapping of more than ninety city-to-province entries; and dates onto ISO format, with Chinese numeral expressions converted to integers.

新增The economic rationale for this layer is explicit. Because it is deterministic and incurs no API cost, it runs on every record without qualification, and its confidence output decides what happens next: a record whose confidence falls below 0.7, or which is missing three or more of the four critical fields, is flagged for enhancement, and the remaining records proceed with rule-derived values. This design keeps the majority of field-level values independent of any model, which is what allows Section 4.3 to compare rule-derived and model-derived fields against an independent annotator.

新增#### 3.2.4 Relevance filtering and deduplication

新增**Relevance filtering.** Not every collected page concerns a construction accident; portals also carry regulatory notices, training announcements, and industrial accidents from mining, chemicals, and transport. Filtering proceeds in two gates, the second of which is described with the pipeline stages.

新增The first gate operates on titles. Twenty-six construction keywords — covering building, municipal works, demolition, scaffolding, tower cranes, foundation pits, formwork, and the standard accident-type terms — admit a title if any one matches, while eight disqualifying patterns reject administrative documents outright (supervision notices, interim measures, management rules, implementation details, training sessions, and the accident-reporting regulation itself). This gate is deliberately permissive: its purpose is to discard obvious non-accidents cheaply, not to decide the borderline cases.

新增Records passing the title gate face a rule-based classifier that scores four signal vocabularies against the title and the opening of the body text. One vocabulary lists roughly thirty terms that occur almost exclusively on construction sites (tower crane, construction hoist, foundation pit works, formwork support, concrete pouring, rebar tying, earthwork excavation, pile foundations). A second lists roughly twenty-eight construction terms that also appear in industrial settings (construction site, building works, scaffolding, work at height, demolition works). A third lists roughly fifty industrial and extractive terms (mining, steel, cement, chemicals, textiles, power). A fourth lists roughly twenty disqualifying terms (road traffic, highway, railway derailment, coal-mine gas, fishing vessels, flood control, hazardous chemicals). Seven ordered rules then combine these signals: specific construction vocabulary admits a record even if an industrial firm is named, an industrial firm with fewer than three construction signals is rejected, a non-industrial firm with two or more construction signals is admitted, and a single construction signal is admitted with low confidence and routed onward rather than decided. The classifier returns a binary judgment with a confidence of *high* or *low*; only high-confidence judgments are final.

新增**Deduplication.** Mirrored and reprinted reports of a single accident are common across Chinese news portals, so a record's provenance is not evidence of its uniqueness. The pipeline applies a four-layer procedure, ordered from cheap and exact to expensive and approximate. Layer 1 compares URL identity, using a hash of the URL with the scheme prefix, `www`, trailing slash, and fragment removed. Layer 2 compares a structural fingerprint assembled from year-month, city, death toll, injury toll, and accident type, hashing to twelve hexadecimal characters; because the fingerprint uses year-month rather than the full date, it tolerates the reporting-date discrepancies of a few days that occur when a portal republishes an investigation report. Layer 3 compares title semantics, encoding titles with a Chinese sentence-embedding model and flagging a pair whose cosine similarity reaches 0.85; the candidate set is restricted to a spatial–temporal bucket keyed on month and province, which keeps the comparison from growing with corpus size. When the embedding model is unavailable, an edit-distance ratio with a threshold of 0.80 substitutes. Layer 4 cross-validates four facts independently of title wording — date proximity, city identity, casualty agreement, and accident-type agreement — and requires at least two checks to pass with a normalized agreement above 0.75.

新增The design intent is that exact and structural layers intercept the large majority of duplicates and only genuinely ambiguous cases reach the semantic layer. When a duplicate is detected, the pipeline does not discard the incoming record. It merges: the new source URL is appended to the existing record, the source counter increments, and any field the existing record lacks is filled from the new one. Forty-five records in the database carry more than one source URL, and the merged set is exposed through the interface, so a user can see that a given finding rests on, or is corroborated by, more than one publication.

新增**Integration.** Accepted records are written to storage under two idempotency rules. Raw candidate text is keyed by a hash of its source URL, so re-running collection over the same source adds nothing and the pipeline can be resumed after interruption. Classification, the most expensive per-record step, checkpoints every ten records, so an interrupted run resumes from the last checkpoint rather than re-issuing model calls. Both properties matter for a corpus of this size assembled by a small team: they make collection and classification restartable without manual bookkeeping.

新增### 3.3 CAICS: an operational coding scheme

新增The Construction Accident Integrated Cause System (CAICS) is a 5-layer, 19-category coding scheme designed for construction accident narratives. Its layers follow the causal leak model: the management layer (M) asks why the site was vulnerable; the protection layer (F) asks why physical barriers failed; the trigger layer (T) asks what material condition initiated the event; the human act layer (H) asks what unsafe behavior preceded it; and the consequence layer (C) asks why the outcome exceeded expectation. The scheme was derived through corpus-driven induction under the leak model: causal statements were extracted from the corpus, clustered into candidate categories, and consolidated into the five layers, with categories below a frequency floor merged or dropped; each retained category received an operational definition and corpus-derived evidence keywords. CAICS is therefore positioned in this paper as an operational scheme for this database — a finite, LLM-oriented coding frame with explicit definitions — not as an established causal theory. 4. Experimental Evaluation

删除The evaluation addresses three questions in sequence: which backbone model should drive the pipeline (Section 4.3), how should that model be prompted for causal coding (Section 4.4), and whether the resulting annotations are trustworthy at the scale of human expert labeling (Section 4.5). Sections 4.1 and 4.2 define the datasets and metrics shared by all three experiments.

删除### 4.1 Datasets and Sampling

删除#### 4.4.3 The Lexical Gatekeeping Effect

删除The initial version of S4 (v1) embedded domain knowledge as conditional checks: each code was presented with an interrogative gate, for example "M1 deficient supervision: does the text mention 'no supervision' or 'supervision failure'?" accompanied by an instruction not to fabricate codes. The outcome was catastrophic: F1 = 0.503, recall = 0.423, 21% of records received no codes at all, and M1 — the most frequent ground-truth code, present in 85 of 100 records — reached a recall of only 0.141. Most accident narratives never use the exact keyword phrases; they say "安全管理不到位" (safety management inadequate) or "未及时发现制止" (failure to detect and stop in time). Under the conditional-check phrasing, the model treated the listed keywords as mandatory gates rather than illustrative cues, and was effectively blind to every paraphrase.

删除We name this failure mode the **Lexical Gatekeeping Effect**: in conditional-check CoT prompts, an LLM converts illustrative keyword lists into necessary conditions for code emission, and paraphrased evidence that fails the literal gate is discarded, producing systematic recall collapse concentrated on the highest-frequency implicit categories.

删除The remedy replaces the gate with a two-step judgment: (1) explicit matching — does the text directly support this code, with any paraphrase? (2) authorized inference — given the accident's nature and construction-safety knowledge, is this cause necessarily present even without explicit phrasing? The conservative instruction was also replaced by a coverage preference appropriate to safety analysis, where missing a cause costs more than adding one. The combined v1→v2 effects were large: F1 rose from 0.503 to 0.656, recall from 0.423 to 0.872, M1 recall from 0.141 to 0.988, and empty outputs fell from 21% to 0. Figure 6 visualizes the recovery. Because the v1→v2 transition changed two prompt properties at once (phrasing and output policy), we isolate their separate contributions with a controlled ablation below.

删除The mechanism behind the effect is instructive. Interrogative phrasing ("does the text mention X?") activates the model's literal pattern-matching mode: attention concentrates on fragments containing the exact keywords, and paraphrases receive low weight. Declarative cue phrasing ("cues include a, b, c; even without these words, infer from the accident's nature") activates the reasoning mode, in which keywords define an open semantic family and the model judges membership autonomously. This asymmetry generalizes beyond construction: in any domain requiring recall-over-precision annotation (safety analysis, medical screening), CoT prompts should phrase conditions as clue guidance rather than condition checks; condition-check phrasing suits zero-false-positive settings such as legal citation.

删除**Controlled ablation.** To decompose the recovery into the contribution of phrasing and the contribution of the output-policy instruction, we ran a 2×2 ablation on the strategy sample itself: v1 (conditional checks + conservative instruction), v2a (two-step judgment + conservative instruction), v2b (conditional checks + coverage preference), and v2 (two-step judgment + coverage preference, the production configuration). 删除#### 4.4.4 Serial layer-by-layer reasoning fails on bidirectional causal chains

删除S5 decomposed the five-layer analysis into five independent API calls, each performed by a layer specialist, with the prior layer's results injected as a summary. The theoretical expectation, following the design of HFACS-CoT+ (Liu et al., 2025a), was that per-layer isolation prevents cross-layer attention dilution and improves per-layer quality. The result rejects the expectation: S5 attains F1 = 0.665, statistically indistinguishable from S4 (0.656, overlapping bootstrap CIs), at six times the API calls (5,430 s vs. 1,843 s for 100 records). Isolation buys nothing here; it merely redistributes the same reasoning across five truncated contexts.

删除The diagnosis is architectural. Each layer specialist receives a lossy summary of upstream findings ("M layer = M1, M2; F layer = F2; T layer = T2") without the reasoning that produced them. More fundamentally, CAICS causal chains are bidirectional: a trigger event (T) exposes a protection failure (F), which traces back to a management deficiency (M). A single integrated call can traverse these links freely within one reasoning context; the serial architecture truncates the chain at every layer boundary, and the truncated contexts evidently suffice to recover most of the coding quality at substantially higher cost. Liu et al. (2025a) found serial decomposition mildly beneficial on GPT-4o for HFACS aviation codes, whose inter-code dependencies are comparatively sparse and well-mapped. Our results suggest that for tightly coupled frameworks such as CAICS, integrated single-call reasoning with an enlarged reasoning budget is the cost-efficient design, and serial decomposition adds no measurable quality.

5. Discussion

删除### 5.1 Theoretical implications

删除The strategy-ranking reversal between our experiments and Liu et al. (2025a) is best read as evidence about prompting, not about models. Few-Shot and domain CoT occupy different points in the space of supervision styles: Few-Shot transmits implicit norms through examples, while domain CoT transmits explicit procedure through instructions. Which style wins depends on the model's capability profile — its in-context pattern-matching efficiency versus its instruction-following alignment — or on the task's structure; our cross-study contrast cannot separate the two, and Section 4.4.2 states that boundary explicitly. A practical corollary: for the open-model family, Few-Shot is the more reliable default; for instruction-tuned commercial models, domain CoT retains its documented advantage. The Lexical Gatekeeping Effect sharpens this lesson by showing that domain CoT is not merely weaker on some models but actively dangerous when its embedded knowledge is phrased as conditional checks: a poorly designed CoT prompt underperforms a blank prompt (v1 F1 = 0.503 versus Zero-Shot 0.600). Our ablation (Table 7) establishes the causal role of phrasing under controlled conditions — the phrasing manipulation alone, with the conservative instruction held constant, delivers 0.62 of the total 0.64 recovery on the flagship implicit category. The asymmetry between interrogative and declarative phrasing generalizes a design rule for structured extraction: phrase conditions as clues when recall dominates (safety, screening) and as checks when false positives dominate (legal, financial). This rule holds for any domain where implicit categories predominate and literal evidence is the exception.

删除### 5.2 Practical implications

删除For safety practice, three consequences follow. First, evidence-bound coding makes every causal annotation traceable to a source sentence, converting the database from a statistical object into an auditable one — safety analysts can verify the grounds of any code without re-reading full reports. Second, the validated agreement levels (κ = 0.91–0.97) justify LLM substitution for expert coding of basic fields at industrial scale, at a marginal cost of USD 0.0007 per record (Section 4.3), which places continuous nationwide monitoring within reach of safety regulators. Third, the MECE enumeration procedure offers a template for standardizing any free-text field in safety reporting; its LLM-first orientation — observe the data, then design the taxonomy — avoids the unmappable-value failures of rule-first schemes, as evidenced by the 18.9% → 2.9% reduction of the residual "other" class in the work-task field.

删除### 5.4 Limitations

删除Seven limitations bound the current work. (1) The strategy comparison ran on a single model; the model-dependence hypothesis rests on cross-study contrast with Liu et al. (2025a) rather than a within-study multi-model design. (2) The agreement validation covers six basic features but not the causal codes themselves; expert ground truth for the strategy experiment (three experts, Fleiss' κ > 0.85) bounds causal-code reliability indirectly, but a direct κ study on causal coding at scale remains future work; correspondingly, the practical substitution claim (Section 5.2) applies to basic fields only. (3) The city-field accuracy (81.5–88.0%) reflects an administrative-level mismatch (county-level cities reported versus prefecture-level ground truth) that a hierarchical location model could resolve. (4) The multimodal modules are display-oriented: image quality at the source constrains what VLM analysis can contribute, and the 282 VLM descriptions are presented as supplementary material rather than verified annotations. (5) The database covers news-reported accidents, which oversample severe incidents relative to the true accident population; generalization to minor incidents requires regulatory report access. (6) All LLM runs used temperature 0.0 with a single execution per configuration; the reported strategy metrics are point estimates without variance, and low-frequency codes (e.g., C1 with one ground-truth occurrence in the strategy sample) have unreliable per-code estimates. (7) The 972-record silver standard was constructed by pipeline-version agreement with rule-based arbitration rather than independent human re-annotation; its basic-field reliability is bounded by the two source versions, and only the 100-record causal subset carries independent expert validation.

新增### 5.1 Implications

新增For data engineering practice, three implications follow from the measured results, each stated within its scope. First, the tiered evidence scheme offers a workable alternative to the all-or-nothing claim that every code must carry verbatim support: prior-inference codes are useful for downstream analysis provided they are stored, displayed, and reported separately, which is what the pipeline does. Second, the phrasing observation generalizes as a design caution: in this model and task, interrogative conditional-check phrasing suppressed paraphrastic evidence for implicit categories, and the two-step judgment phrasing recovered most of it (0.800 of the total 0.847 M1-recall recovery). Practitioners designing recall-critical extraction prompts should test phrasing variants rather than assume that listed keywords behave as illustrative cues. Third, the agreement results justify a limited claim: for six basic fields, one LLM configuration and one trained annotator reached high protocol agreement, which supports using the LLM to reduce — not replace — human labeling effort for those fields under human supervision.

新增### 5.3 Limitations

新增We list the limitations that bound the main conclusions, organized by their severity.

新增(1) *Reference independence.* The 972-record silver standard is partly model-derived (pipeline-version agreement with rule arbitration); only the 100-record causal subset carries independent three-expert validation. Conclusions drawn on the silver standard inherit this bound, and a fully independent human gold standard — with a published annotation guide, annotator backgrounds, blinding, arbitration rules, and per-class sample sizes — remains future work.

新增(2) *Development–test overlap.* The 100-record strategy sample served prompt development (v1 diagnosis, v2 design, ablation) and final reporting simultaneously, and the three few-shot examples were not isolated from the test records by an explicit procedure. The strategy results therefore carry development-set reuse risk and should be read as a development evaluation; a frozen test set separated from all prompt-design decisions is planned for the next iteration.

新增(3) *Prompt-phrasing inference.* The four-cell comparison reports simple pairwise differences on one sample with single runs; it does not estimate formal interaction effects, and it cannot support claims about model-internal mechanisms. The Lexical Gatekeeping Effect is reported as an observation with a practical remedy, not as an isolated causal mechanism.

新增(4) *Model comparison conditions.* The four models ran under unequal conditions (enforced temperature, output budget, tier routing), each configuration ran once, and model identifiers were recorded at family granularity. The comparison supports an adoption decision under these constraints, not a general capability ranking.

新增(5) *Agreement scope.* The agreement study covers one annotator, six of 33 fields, and no independent arbitration; the causal codes lack a large-sample agreement study. The results support effort reduction on basic fields under supervision, not expert substitution.

新增(6) *Database audit.* The corpus has known quality gaps: the classifier's residual multi-accident errors are not fully audited, the four-layer deduplication of mirrored reports described in Section 3.2.4 has not been followed by an independent estimate of residual duplication, field missingness varies widely across fields (from 13.7% to 83.9% non-null on the five enum fields), and the corpus overrepresents severe accidents because its sources are public news portals and official investigation reports. The database covers publicly retrievable accident coverage; it is not a representative sample of all construction accidents.

新增(7) *Data governance.* The pipeline collects and plans to redistribute text and images from news portals and government sites. Public accessibility does not by itself settle re-publication rights; per-source permissions, takedown mechanisms, and access controls for traumatic imagery are discussed in Section 7 and require completion before any public release.

新增(8) *Reproducibility.* Data, code, and full prompts are released upon publication rather than at submission; single-run configurations and family-level model identifiers limit exact reproduction. We commit to releasing an anonymous read-only database, verbatim prompts, a minimal reproduction package, and a system demo before resubmission.

6. Conclusion

删除This paper presented CAIEMS, an end-to-end LLM-driven pipeline that converts unstructured Chinese construction accident narratives into a structured, causally annotated, interactive online database. Three results carry the contribution. First, the CAICS five-layer, nineteen-category taxonomy provides a construction-specific causal substrate, with 17,364 evidence-bound codes across 2,623 records. Second, the Lexical Gatekeeping Effect — identified, diagnosed, and remedied through a two-step judgment design (F1 0.503 → 0.656) — establishes a generalizable prompt-design rule, while the Few-Shot victory over domain CoT (0.669 vs. 0.656) demonstrates that prompting-strategy rankings are model-dependent. Third, the agreement study certifies LLM basic-field annotations at expert inter-rater levels (κ = 0.91–0.97, n = 289). Future work extends along three lines: a within-study multi-model test of the model-dependence hypothesis, direct agreement validation for causal coding at scale, and evaluation of the multimodal descriptions once higher-quality accident imagery becomes available.

新增This paper reported CAIEMS, an exploratory LLM-driven pipeline that converts publicly available Chinese construction accident narratives into a queryable database prototype: 2,623 records drawn from web portals and published case compilations, 33 fields, and 16,323 causal annotations organized in a three-tier evidence scheme, served through a Web interface and REST API. Three supporting experiments, each with stated scope, anchor the reliability discussion: a constrained four-model comparison that motivated the adoption of DeepSeek V4 (91.2% basic-field accuracy at the lowest cost), a six-strategy prompt comparison that surfaced the Lexical Gatekeeping observation and its two-step judgment remedy, and a protocol study showing high agreement between the LLM and one trained annotator on six basic fields. The boundaries of these results are stated throughout: the silver standard is partly model-derived, development and test samples overlap, and no causal mechanism or expert substitution is claimed. The next iteration of this work follows a defined sequence: an independent human gold standard for the causal codes, a frozen test set separated from prompt development, a formal factorial design for the phrasing question, multi-model repetition under matched conditions, and a completed data audit with governance before any public release.
